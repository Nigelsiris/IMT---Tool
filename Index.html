About Invoice Finalization

Invoice Finalization allows you to review and manually adjust GL code allocations after invoice extraction but before final PDF export and saving.

  • Load and review pending invoices in this view
  • Edit existing GL code amounts by clicking on the amount field
  • Add new GL codes from pre-configured templates or enter custom codes
  • Link GL codes to other cost centers (e.g., take from store deliveries)
  • The total of all GL codes must equal the invoice amount
  • Finalize to apply GL coding and generate PDFs
